AI risk management:

Backtest trades per strategy:
Tick "Limit backtest trades" and set the number: every strategy then takes up to that many backtest trades. Untick for unlimited (every signal trades). AI auto trades overrides the manual limit when enabled. "Deduct broker charges" subtracts charges per closed trade: Dhan auto computes the real Dhan cost (₹20/order brokerage, 0.1% STT on sell, 0.03503% NSE exchange, 0.0001% SEBI, 0.003% stamp duty on buy, IPFT, 18% GST), Custom uses your flat ₹ per trade - showing gross P&L, the charge, and net per trade.
Daily chart basis:
Runs the backtest over that many historical days and groups results per IST day. With "Limit backtest trades" + "per-day" ticked, up to that many trades execute EVERY day (e.g. 20/day x 5 days = 100). Details show each day's P&L, win rate and reward:risk, plus the overall win rate & RR.
Risk:Reward:
When on, every trade's profit target = Stop-Loss % x Target RR (e.g. SL 1% x RR 2 = 2% target), overriding manual/AI TP. The daily breakdown also highlights each day's realised reward:risk (green = met, orange = missed).
