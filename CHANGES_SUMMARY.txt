   wire the onFastestRisingInput handler + fastestCount selection logic.

   gates in BOTH engines (aismart.js + autoexperiment.v13.js).

     SMF, RSI, OBV, Fisher, Aroon, Vortex, ADX (+DI/-DI pair)) each evaluated
     as a main-line vs signal-line level-hold gate with cmpType 'self'.
   - Rows injected into the AST + AE bullish/bearish filter panels under a new
     "Multi-Line Momentum Gap (level - main line above signal and gap
     holding/widening up/down)" header (templates/index.html

     11 indicators keep the plain two-line gap gate.
