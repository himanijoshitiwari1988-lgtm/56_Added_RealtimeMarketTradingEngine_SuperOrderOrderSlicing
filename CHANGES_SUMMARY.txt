2) AST paper-poll entry decision now runs directly on the real-time chart
   candles (static/aismart.js tickBody per-instrument loop).
   - The option-chain execution-target resolution (executionSymbolsFor /
     contractsFor) and the REST quote/candle subscription (ensureOptionQuotes)
     no longer run BEFORE every per-instrument condition check. They were moved
     out of the decision path and are deferred to just after a fresh entry
     signal fires - immediately before the order is placed - so a condition
     that meets on the live chart places the paper entry on the next poll
     without ever waiting on chain resolution / quote subscription. The
     "waiting for entry signal" state and every non-firing instrument now do
     zero chain / REST-quote work per poll.
   - Open-position management for a strategy+instrument now reads the paper
     engine's own positions directly (positions whose autoKey === key) instead
     of iterating chain-derived trade targets (posKeyOf over executionSymbols
     targets), so even a held trade no longer triggers a chain fetch every
     poll. The one-trade-per-signal latch (armed while a position of ours is
     open) and the existing exit/trail management behaviour are preserved.
   - The realtime evaluation still reads the same chart/FastLive candles as
     before; only the ordering relative to chain/quote work changed. The HFT
     scanner path is unaffected (it already resolved targets synchronously
     from cache).
